# HeyNews

HeyNews builds AI tools for newsletter creators, publishers, and company teams. Our main product, [HeyNews](https://heynews.co/), learns your editorial voice from past issues and helps you curate stories, write drafts, and edit your next newsletter. You choose the sources and stories, review the draft, and decide what to publish.

## How HeyNews helps you publish

1. **Learn your voice.** Connect your newsletter archive so an AI Writer can learn your tone, structure, and recurring editorial patterns.
2. **Choose your material.** Follow sources, collect links, and select the stories for each issue. Use AI story selection when you want help narrowing the list.
3. **Draft and edit.** Generate an issue, refine the copy, and prepare a subject line and preview text.
4. **Review and publish.** You make the editorial decisions and send through your newsletter platform. Scheduled HeyNews automations prepare drafts for review.

HeyNews connects with beehiiv and Kit for newsletter history and performance data, and can learn from other newsletter archives. Visit the [knowledge base](https://roadmap.heynews.co/t/knowledge-base) for setup guides and current capabilities.

For companies that want help launching or running a newsletter, we also offer managed newsletter services. [Talk with our team about your publication](https://heynews.co/talk-to-us).
